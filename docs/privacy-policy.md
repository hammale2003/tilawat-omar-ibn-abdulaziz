# Privacy Policy — تلاوات عمر بن عبد العزيز

**Last updated: 12 November 2026**

## The short version

This app collects nothing. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising,
and no tracking of any kind.

## What the app stores

Everything the app remembers is stored only on your own device, and is deleted
when you uninstall the app:

- which recitation you last listened to, and where you had reached in it
- surahs you have bookmarked
- your playback settings (speed, repeat mode, theme)
- any audio files you chose to download for offline listening

None of this is ever transmitted anywhere. We cannot see it.

## Network use

The app connects to the internet for one purpose only: to fetch the recitation
audio files when you stream or download a surah. These files are hosted on the
Internet Archive (archive.org). No personal information is sent with these
requests beyond what any ordinary file download involves.

The Internet Archive has its own privacy policy, which governs those requests:
https://archive.org/about/terms.php

## Permissions

| Permission | Why |
|---|---|
| Internet | to fetch recitation audio |
| Foreground service / wake lock | to keep playing when the screen is off |
| Post notifications | to show the playback controls on the lock screen |

The app requests no access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone,
photos, files, or any other personal data.

## Children

The app contains no advertising, no in-app purchases, no user-generated content
and no external links. It collects no data from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be published at this same
address with a new date at the top.

## Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the developer through the app's Play
Store or App Store listing.
